ENCOUNTER SCRIPT
Generated by `npm run dialogue` from the game's encounter files. Edit the words freely, then send it back (or push it) and the
changes are put into the encounters. Lines are only the words that are read aloud.

HOW IT IS LAID OUT
  ===== TITLE (encounter_id) =====   one encounter
  [stage_id]                         one screen of the encounter. Leave these tags as they are: they say where the words go.
  plain text                         what the narrator reads (and what is shown)
  > Choice label                     a button (shown, never read). Its own line under it, if any, is read when it is picked.

MARKS YOU CAN ADD
  [pause]          a beat of silence in the reading, about a second   e.g.  The door creaks. [pause] Nothing moves.
  [pause 3]        a pause of that many seconds
  ---              on a line of its own inside a stage: a stage break. What follows becomes the next screen, behind a
                   "Continue." button (as with Darius's silent "......")
  (( words ))      shown on screen but not read aloud
  NAME: "words"    read by that character's voice, not the narrator's (one voice per name: DARIUS, MERCHANT, ...)

Write "you" for the player: a recorded voice cannot say a name the player chose.

===== A STRANGE OBELISK (strange_obelisk) =====

[discovery]
Your party discovers a towering obelisk of smooth, black stone. Strange geometries are carved into its surface, and they seem to shift when not directly observed.
  > Continue.

[discovery_2]

  > [INT] Decipher the shifting glyphs.
  > [STR] Excavate the earth around its base.
  > [DEX] Examine its surface and seams.
  > Lay a hand on the stone and let it unwrite you.
      You lay a palm flat against the cold stone...
  > Switch party member.
  > Leave the obelisk alone.
      Sensing danger, you decide it is best to leave this ancient stone undisturbed.

[decipher_success]
The shifting lines suddenly make perfect sense. They recount the early days of the Altus Kingdom, and the knowledge settles into your mind and changes who you are.
  > Take what the stone has taught you.

[decipher_fail]
The geometries twist in impossible ways. It hurts to look at. A sharp pain shoots behind your eyes, and something in you is left altered.
  > Stumble away from the stone.

[excavate_success]
Beneath the soil, the obelisk's foundation is carved with row upon row of tools, scenes and figures. Working them free, you learn of a strange people group, in a world backward your own.
  > Take what the stone has taught you.

[excavate_fail]
The earth round the base is packed hard as iron. The stone shifts, grinds, and bites, and by the time you pull free you are bruised, winded, and not quite the same.
  > Give up the dig.

[examine_success]
One seam is not a seam at all: a panel slides aside under your fingers and, inside, a pattern of carvings that teaches the hand something the mind cannot name.
  > Take what the stone has taught you.

[examine_fail]
A seam snaps shut on your hand and a dull ache runs up the arm. Something about the stone has left a mark that will not fade.
  > Pull away from the stone.

[unmaking]
The glyphs crawl toward your hand and the stone grows warm. Whatever you have become, for better or worse, it will be unwritten. Every trait, the good with the bad. The stone will not be used twice.
  > Let it take everything.
      You do not pull away. The glyphs sink into your skin and are gone...
  > Pull your hand away.
