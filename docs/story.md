# Shackle Breaker: story reference

The story the game and the book (*Shackle Breaker*, by Owen Walstrom, unfinished) share. Check new dialogue, quest text and encounters against
this, so the game never contradicts the book or gives a secret away early. **Secrets are marked; never let a line reveal them before the story does.**

**The book covers the 3 to 5 years leading up to the game**: the artifact, the court, the road to war. **The game takes place after it**, after the
fall, when the player breaks out of the king's chain line. This page is a living document: add to it and change it as the story and the game grow.

## The world

- **The Altus Kingdom** (Greek naming): the kingdom of the Altus dynasty. Its people are **Alterians**. Capital with the **Altus Keep**: outer walls
  around a mountain, a long climb to the inner walls, the **Great Hall** (thirty granite columns, five rows of six, open to the sky) and the palace.
  Rich and festive in the years after the artifact was found. Ruled by four regional governors and the king, each with a fifth of the vote.
- **Panzeria** (German naming): the Altus Kingdom's oldest ally, in the snowy **Panzerian mountains** to the northeast, above the **Bleached Sea**
  (milky white; pink like a pearl at sunset). **Panzerians** are big, tundra-born master engineers: armor with blades, wrist crossbows, spring
  boots, gauntlets; swords forged with the Panzerian salt geysers. Few of them are left. Their symbol is a pouncing tiger. Tensions with Altus grow
  over the artifact.
- **The Namuh** ("human" backwards): a people from **another plane**, who appeared at the start of the war. Nocturnal; they speak in sign language
  and the smallest body language. The artifact drains their realm to power the magic of Altus. **Most of them are good**: they want to destroy the
  artifact before it frees Dolos, to prevent a great catastrophe they know of from a time long past. Their elite are the **nightblades**.
- **The Infernus Kingdom**: an ancient kingdom **below the Altus Kingdom**, of the **ancestors of the Alterians**. Long before the book, it had the
  artifact too: its people went mad with it and the kingdom was destroyed. Altus is repeating its history without knowing it. The catacombs under the
  mysterious altar lead down toward it, and the **Infernus Sentinel** guards the doorway (see below).
  - *Likely link, to confirm:* the "great catastrophe from a time long past" the Namuh know of is the fall of Infernus.
  - *Open question:* how the artifact came back to the surface (the meteor shower) after Infernus fell with it.
- **The Shackle Breakers**: runaways from the king's prisons, bound in magical chains. Rumoured to be a myth. The player is one.

## The stones, the artifact and magic

- **The artifact** is the first and **brightest** stone. It came **before the war**, found after a meteor shower some 3 to 5 years before the story
  opens. The king called it a gift and built a palace and a shrine to it.
- **The other stones kept falling all through the war**, one here, one there, year after year. (Not all on one night.)
- **Magic** came with the artifact. It is like a science, neither good nor bad: the energy for a spell must come **from the caster or from someone
  else**. The artifact draws it from the Namuh's realm; in the game, every spell also takes a piece of the one who casts it (corruption, the shackles).
- Those who use the artifact's power go wild-eyed and laugh without end. People who never use it are mocked as **"lightweights"**.
- **SECRET: Dolos**, an ancient being, is bound inside the artifact and slowly working his way out. He corrupts the king.

## People

- **Cornelius Mentoras**: commander of the royal guard, a royal adviser. Early 40s, observant, disciplined, blunt, guilt-stricken. Broadsword;
  later a chained sword bound to his wrist. His refrain: **"Good enough"** / "Not good enough". Husband of Gwen, father of Jacob and a younger son.
  Friend of the king (once). Rival of Jaeger, friend of Nikolai.
  - **SECRET: he is the Hooded Stranger** of the game (to be revealed later). Shackled, further along, tired, gallows humour; he hears voices.
- **Gwen Mentoras**: his wife. Joyful, wise, a hunter and healer, later a ranger; bow and arrows. After the Namuh attack she flees with their
  younger son to the rangers and searches for Cornelius.
- **The Mentoras sons**: two. **Jacob** is the elder; the **younger son** has no name yet (he flees with Gwen to the rangers).
- **Jacob Mentoras**: their elder son, dead five years when the book opens. On the expedition that brought the artifact back, the artifact's dark power
  raised the dead and killed him. Mentoras's last words to him were harsh: "Not good enough."
  - **SECRET: Dolos is Jacob** (revealed at the end).
- **The king**: called **Saul** in the book's chapters and **Belshazzar Altus** in the outline (the game never names him yet). Kind at first, slowly
  corrupted by the artifact and Dolos. Uses its magic (lights and quells fire). Turns on Mentoras after the attack on the capital; goes mad; the
  kingdom falls. **SECRET: he becomes Dolos's puppet.**
- **Darius**: **regional governor** of the central province and **one of the king's advisers**. Arrogant, entitled, a flatterer of the powerful,
  loves dramatic entrances and name-calling ("Lightweight"). The artifact's eager champion. In the game: the king gave him **one of the stones** (not
  the artifact) to keep safe until he came back; he lost it, sent his soldiers in the old colors to search for it forever, and kneels at the altar
  in the caves waiting for a king who never comes. When he sees the glow of the player's stone he takes it for the king's sign that the stranger in
  chains was entrusted too, and turns on them. (Darius the Groveler, the end of act 1.)
- **Jaeger Ironson, "The Pursuer"**: Panzerian general, Mentoras's analytical rival; ruthless (a hunt where his four companions froze to death).
  Spring boots, gauntlets. Later corrupted, sent to hunt the escaped Mentoras.
- **Nikolai Ironson**: Jaeger's brother, Panzerian general. Witty, boyish grin, missing teeth, speaks in the third person in broken Alterian, yet
  educated at both a Panzerian and an Alterian university. Mentoras's friend. Later joins him and Codey.
- **Elsali / El Shadi, "Codey"**: a Namuh nightblade, late teens. Mute, empathetic, quick, a scrapper. Fought Mentoras to a standstill in the attack on
  the capital; imprisoned with him; saves him in the escape; nicknamed "Codey" for his hand signs. Protagonist 2.
- **General Sekrav**: general of the Alterian legion. Young, never defeated, popular.
- **Director Kokul**: director of Artifact Affairs.
- **Shmindolyn** and **Kurtucus**: legends. (The game's Kurtus' Brew can nod to Kurtucus.)

## The story, in order

1. The artifact is found after a meteor shower. An expedition (Mentoras, the king, Darius, Jacob) brings it home; Jacob dies to its dark power.
2. Years of plenty and festivity in Altus; magic spreads; the first madness. Tensions with Panzeria over the artifact (the book's chapters 1 and 2).
3. The war. The Namuh appear and, with Panzeria, attack the capital ("the battle beneath no banner") to destroy the artifact. Mentoras and a
   nightblade fight to a standstill; the king breaks it up with the artifact's magic and binds the nightblade and a Panzerian general in magical
   chains. Mentoras scolds the king and tries to destroy the artifact; the king stops him and sends him to prison with them.
4. The king sends Jaeger after Gwen and her younger son; they escape to the rangers.
5. **The fall of the Altus Kingdom**: the people go mad from gluttony and magic; the mad fill the prisons, then the kingdom becomes mindless
   hollows. Dolos rules through the king.
6. Years later, Mentoras breaks out with Codey (the Shackle Breakers). Jaeger, now the Pursuer, hunts them. Nikolai joins them.
7. The end: Dolos is revealed as Jacob, blames Mentoras for everything; a last battle; Mentoras offers his son redemption, Dolos uses it to wound
   him mortally, and is defeated. Codey finds Mentoras and calls him Dad; Mentoras calls him son and dies at peace.

## How the game follows it

- The player is a nameless prisoner in the king's chain line who breaks free (the prologue): a Shackle Breaker, after the fall.
- The madmen, magic addicts, rotters and hollows are the people of Altus gone mad; the legionaries and watchmen in "the old colors" still march as
  if the war never ended (Darius's soldiers, searching for his stone).
- The catacombs (act 2) end in a dark round hall where the **Infernus Sentinel** stands: a living stone statue of a cosmic knight, cracks in its armour
  open onto a moving night sky. It guards the doorway down to Infernus. What the game says so far is short and cryptic: waking, *"Again the light. Again the fall."*; broken, *"Below, they remember."* That Infernus were the Alterians' own ancestors, driven mad by the same
  artifact, is **not stated yet**: keep it for when the story goes down there.
- Act 1 ends with Darius; act 2 is the blood moon. Corruption is the game's cost of magic; the shackles take those who cast too much.
